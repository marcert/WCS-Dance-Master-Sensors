# Solo Training — Quickstart ⚡

> No patience for a manual? Start here. Two minutes to your first useful feedback.
> The full walkthrough waits in the **[Dancer's Guide — Solo](dancer_guide_solo.md)** whenever you want it.

---

## 1. Beginner — dancing in 60 seconds

**Set up once:**

1. Prop your phone in **landscape** at eye level, facing you.
2. Open **`http://192.168.4.1/solo`** in the browser.
3. Tap **`📷 CAM`** (you appear behind the cards), then stand still in your dance shoes — feet flat, weight even — and tap **`📐 ZERO`**.
4. Tap **`🔊 Biofeedback: OFF`** to turn the beeps **ON**. The beep is your coach.

**Your only job:** land softly — **heel first going forward, toe first going back.**

**Watch one card (bottom-right).** After every step it shows:

| You see | Meaning | What to do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `HEEL STRIKE ✓` or `TOE-FIRST ✓` (green) | Clean landing | 👍 Keep going |
| A **beep** + red badge (`HEEL SLAM` / `TOE JAM` / `HARD IMPACT`) | You landed too hard | Slow down. Bend the knee, land softer. |
| `—` (grey) on a step you meant backward | Foot landed too flat | Send the toe out first, let the heel arrive last. |

Walk forward and back to the music until the badge is green on almost every step. **Then speed up.**

> 💡 **One thing at a time.** Ignore every other card and number for now — they unlock later.

---

## 2. Got it? Here's what's next 🏃

When green comes easily at your normal tempo, tap **`👤 BEG`** once to switch to **`🏃 INT`**. Three new things to aim for:

- **ROLL badge** → `CLEAN ROLL ✓`: lower the front of your foot under control — don't let it slap down.
- **Double Stance card** → `OPTIMAL ROLL`: roll through the foot; don't lift one before the other is planted.
- **DELAY badge** (on anchor steps) → `DELAYED ✓`: let your weight arrive a beat *after* your foot touches, not at the same instant.

Nail these three and you've outgrown the quickstart.
